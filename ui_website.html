About Inkuthazo

A social club designed for real-life goals.

Inkuthazo started as a small group of friends pooling money to support each other's goals. Today, it is a structured social club with clear rules, elected leaders and a growing record of helping members reach their financial milestones.

Our purpose

We exist to encourage disciplined saving, financial education and mutual support. Members contribute monthly into a shared fund which is paid out in turns, giving everyone a chance to tackle large expenses without taking on debt.

How we operate

We follow a constitution, record contributions, and share reports with the group. Leadership is elected, decisions are made transparently, and all funds are managed through an agreed banking channel for safety and accountability.

How it works

Simple, transparent contribution cycles.

Every member plays a part. Contributions, payout order and rules are all agreed upfront and shared with the group.

1. Build the circle

A group of committed members agrees on contribution amounts, payout order and cycle length.

2. Contribute monthly

Members deposit their agreed amount every month into the club account before the deadline.

3. Receive your payout

Each month, one or more members receive the full pot according to the agreed rotation schedule.

4. Support & accountability

Late payments, emergencies and changes are handled through clear rules and group decisions.

Typical contribution plans

Most members choose between R500, R1000 or R1500 per month depending on their goals and budget.
