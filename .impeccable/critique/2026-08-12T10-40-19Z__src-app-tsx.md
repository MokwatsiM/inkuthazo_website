Nine section headings share one byte-identical class. Identical section rhythm means Membership gets the same air as Gatherings. ## Priority Issues

### [P0] Four primary CTA buttons fail contrast (measured, worse on hover)
White on primary-500 (#f59e0b) = 2.15:1, fails AA and AA-large. Hover shifts to primary-400 = 1.67:1. Affects Navigation.tsx:78, Navigation.tsx:112, JoinUs.tsx:84, Contact.tsx:130 — including "Complete application", the actual conversion button. In bright outdoor light on mid-range Android this is near-unreadable.
Fix: drop gradient on primary actions, use solid bg-stone-900 (17.49:1). One style, one label.
Note: accent-600 on stone-50 computes 4.4966 — displays as 4.50 but is BELOW threshold. Marginal fail. Site-wide eyebrow colour.
Command: /impeccable colorize

### [P1] The R200 contradiction
Membership.tsx states flat R200/month. HowItWorks.tsx:20 says "agree on a monthly contribution that fits your circumstances… affordable and flexible". Pillars.tsx:29 repeats it. Visitor cannot tell if R200 is the price or an opening offer.
Command: /impeccable clarify

### [P1] Waiting period filed as a benefit, never explained
Membership.tsx benefits[4], styled identically to four pieces of good news. Repeated in 12px fine print adjacent to "amounts may be reviewed at a general meeting" — visual signature of a catch. Never says WHY six months exists (stops deathbed enrolment draining a 20-member pool = fairness to existing members).
Fix: promote to a notice beneath the cost card, above Apply, with the reason. Add arithmetic: R2,400/yr against up to R22,000 cover for household of four.
Command: /impeccable clarify

### [P1] Two credibility leaks
"TBC / TBC" live at Gatherings.tsx:23-24 on a page asking R550; component already has a better empty state. Contact.tsx:113 "Sunday: Closed" vs line 117 "support team available 24/7" — self-contradiction, and peak-end means the final impression is an office-hours table on a site about burying family.
Fix: delete TBC row; delete hours card and 24/7 line; end on the human promise.
Command: /impeccable clarify

### [P2] Ten sections, four making the same argument, eleven CTAs
Pillars(6) + MemberExperience(3) + About grid(4) all answer "what do I get"; "Personal development" verbatim in two. Stats print twice (Hero.tsx:91-95, Pillars.tsx:77-85). Eleven CTAs in four styles under five labels; four are anchor-jumps to #join which asks you to choose again; two point backwards up the page.
Fix: collapse to six sections, promote Membership to position 3, delete MemberExperience and duplicated stats.
Command: /impeccable distill

## Persona Red Flags
